Case study // Retail / Orange County

One standard for every location.

A growing Orange County retailer needed technology support that could scale without making every new location a custom project.

The situation

Each location had accumulated slightly different devices, vendors, and workarounds. New-hire setup and urgent issues depended too heavily on local memory.

The work

FinishLine created a repeatable site baseline, centralized access and device expectations, clarified vendor escalation, and documented the recovery path for the systems that mattered most.

The result

The operator gained a consistent support model, faster onboarding, and a shared view of what was healthy across the footprint—without forcing a wholesale technology replacement.
