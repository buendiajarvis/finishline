Case study // Finance / Newport Beach

Less ticket friction. More operating time.

A Newport Beach finance business wanted dependable support without adding more work to its internal team.

85% routine tickets automated40% cloud spend reduction

The situation

Routine requests were consuming senior attention, while cloud costs and ownership were difficult to see clearly. The business needed better response without sacrificing control.

The work

FinishLine mapped recurring requests, introduced carefully scoped automation, tightened escalation paths, and reviewed cloud usage against actual business needs.

The result

Automation resolved 85% of routine tickets without human intervention. A cloud cost review reduced spend by 40%, while the team gained a clearer path for exceptions and higher-risk requests.
