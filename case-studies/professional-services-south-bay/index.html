Case study // Professional services / South Bay

Security that became someone’s job.

A South Bay professional services team had tools in place, but no shared operating picture or clear recovery owner.

The situation

Access, devices, backups, and response steps had grown organically. The team knew security mattered but could not quickly show what was covered or who acted next.

The work

FinishLine documented the environment, reviewed privileged access, standardized endpoint and backup checks, and translated response steps into an owner-based runbook.

The result

Leadership gained a current baseline, named owners, and a repeatable monthly review. The team moved from “we think so” to evidence it could act on.
