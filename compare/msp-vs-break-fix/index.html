A clear comparison

FinishLine MSP vs. break-fix IT.

Neither model is universally right. The useful question is which one matches your risk, pace, and need for accountable ownership.

DimensionFinishLine MSPBreak-fix technician
Best forTeams that need predictable support, security, and a roadmap.Small or low-dependency environments needing occasional help.
Pricing factorsUsers, devices, coverage, security baseline, and strategic scope.Hourly/project time, urgency, travel, and the incident itself.
ResponseDefined priority levels, service ownership, and proactive monitoring.Often based on availability when the issue appears.
CybersecurityBaseline controls, visibility, maintenance, and readiness are planned together.May be addressed during a project or after an incident.
LimitationsRecurring commitment and less flexibility for very occasional needs.Knowledge can be episodic; costs and downtime are harder to predict.

The decision test

If a day of downtime, an access mistake, or a missed renewal would materially affect the business, predictable ownership is usually worth evaluating.

If your technology footprint is simple and interruptions are genuinely rare, a trusted break-fix relationship may be enough.
