PanelDivider — One-pixel hairline in the outline-variant colour, for separating stacked rows inside a panel.

## Usage

```tsx
<ContactPanel header="Submit a ticket">
  <FormField label="Name"><FormInput /></FormField>
  <PanelDivider />
  <FormNote>A real engineer responds in under 15 minutes.</FormNote>
</ContactPanel>
```
