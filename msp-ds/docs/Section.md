Section — Vertical rhythm band — 96px of padding above and below, with an optional sunken background.

## Usage

```tsx
<Section sunken>
  <Container>
    <SectionLabel>Approach</SectionLabel>
    <h2>How an engagement runs.</h2>
  </Container>
</Section>
```

## Notes

Alternate `sunken` sections to separate one band from the next without drawing a border. Sunken paints `--surface-container-lowest`, one step darker than the page.
